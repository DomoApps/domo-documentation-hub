Hello everyone!

We're excited to announce that version 2.21.0 of the Domo Documentation Hub is now available! This release brings two brand-new connector articles, a friendlier card export experience, a batch of connector refinements, and fresh microlearning videos. You can view these changes in our knowledge base at [www.domo.com/docs](https://www.domo.com/docs).

## 🌟 What's New

### 🔌 Two New Data Connectors

Two new connector articles joined the knowledge base this release. The [Google BigQuery OAuth Writeback Connector](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/1500011337961) documents how to write data from Domo back into BigQuery using OAuth authentication, and the [Emma Connector](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/360043431173) covers pulling your Emma email marketing data into Domo DataSets.

Thanks to Jared Peterson for migrating and publishing both articles.

### 📤 Simpler Card Exports

We updated [Export Visualization Cards](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/360043437813) to remove the outdated 4MB file-size limit note and clean up the export guidance so the steps and notes read clearly. Admins and analysts get more accurate expectations for what they can export.

Thanks to Dany Salas for the correction.

### 🔧 Connector Refinements

Several existing connector articles were sharpened for accuracy:

- The CloudHealth connector was renamed to [CloudHealth Advanced](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/360043433213) to match the current product name, with the intro and prerequisites updated throughout.
- The Freshservice V2 article had its report reference table and FAQ endpoints repaired, including a corrected endpoint path and API domain placeholder.
- Small formatting and reference fixes landed on the BitBucket V2 and Cvent Supplier Network - Planners articles.

Thanks to Arun Raj for the connector cleanup.

### 🎬 New Microlearning Videos

Three new microlearning videos were synced into the [Domo Video Library](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/Domo-Video-Library): setting up and subscribing to Card and Dataset Alerts, and choosing when to use Adrenaline dataflows in SQL-based dataflows. These short walkthroughs help customers get hands-on quickly.

## 🙏 Thank You

A heartfelt thank you to everyone who contributed to this release. Your steady improvements — big and small — keep our documentation accurate and genuinely helpful for customers.

If you have questions or feedback, please reach out — we're always happy to help!
