Hello everyone!

We're excited to announce that version 2.4.0 of the Domo Documentation Hub is now available! This release headlines a major Cloud Integrations documentation overhaul — including a brand-new Data Freshness and Caching article and a cleaner navigation structure for our cloud-database connectors — plus a new Podium OAuth Connector article, expanded SCIM provisioning guidance, and a steady round of connector article polish. You can view these changes in our knowledge base at www.domo.com/docs.


🌟 What's New

🌥️ Cloud Integrations Documentation Overhaul
A focused effort to reshape how customers learn Cloud Integrations:
  • The Cloud Integrations Overview article was significantly streamlined — over 1,000 lines of dense, duplicated content were trimmed away and replaced with a tighter overview backed by seven fresh screenshots that match the current product UI.
  • A brand-new dedicated KB article, "Configuring Data Freshness and Caching in Cloud Integrations," now lives on its own page so admins can find freshness and caching guidance without scrolling through the full overview. The article includes step-by-step enablement instructions, interval configuration, and a grouped FAQ section covering common questions.
  • The cloud-database section of the left-hand nav was restructured to give Snowflake, Google BigQuery, and Databricks their own dedicated groups — making it much easier to navigate the dozens of articles in each family.
Thanks to Felipe Suarez for authoring the new Cloud Integrations content and screenshots, and to Ben Stein for the navigation restructure.

🆕 New Podium OAuth Connector Article
A new KB article for the Podium OAuth Connector is now available, with a full setup walkthrough for the OAuth credentialing flow.
Thanks to ArunRaj for authoring and shepherding the article through review.

🔐 SCIM Provisioning — Okta & Entra Guidance
The Domo SCIM 2.0 article (000005241) picked up several improvements aimed at customers configuring provisioning from Okta or Microsoft Entra:
  • New side-by-side custom-attribute mapping examples for both Okta (`customAttributes.^[key == building].values`) and Entra (`urn:ietf:params:scim:schemas:extension:custom:2.0:User:customAttributes[key eq "building"].value`) to remove a common point of confusion.
  • A new FAQ section covering two well-known Okta gotchas — group push assignment-vs-membership behavior, and what happens when you re-point an Okta app at a different Domo instance.
A small escaping fix also landed in the OpenAPI authentication YAML to clean up rendering on the API reference pages.
Thanks to Jesse Kreitzman for both the SCIM expansion and the authentication YAML fix.

🔌 Connector Article Polish
A broad sweep of targeted improvements across many connector articles:
  • Microsoft SharePoint Online Rest connector — added a new "Disable 404 error warnings" field.
  • Asana connector — added a Survey ID Selection Criteria entry plus several previously missing fields in the Details section.
  • Google Marketing Platform / DV360 connector — added a "Skip failing advertisers" checkbox and several missing details fields, plus a missing Report Table.
  • Limble CMMS connector — removed the obsolete Tasks Part report and re-sorted the reports list in ascending order.
  • CSV / file connectors — added the "Generate Header" field to the Advanced Pane section and updated descriptions.
  • SharePoint and related connectors — added the new "List Items (Fields Only)" and "List Columns" reports, with API endpoint updates.
  • Credential & prerequisite tweaks for token-based authentication on a connector that recently moved to token auth.
  • Multiple connector article report tables re-sorted into ascending order for easier scanning, and an outdated "Crime Report" entry removed from one article.
  • Title tweak on KB article 000005906.
Thanks to ArunRaj for the sustained, detail-level work across this entire batch of connector articles.

🧱 App Framework Design Guide — React Compatibility
The App Framework design guide was refactored to use `className` in place of `class` throughout its code samples — bringing the examples in line with React conventions so customers can copy-paste without hitting JSX warnings — and unused permalink metadata was removed for a cleaner page.
Thanks to Jonathan Tiritilli for the refactor.
