## Intro

This article explains how to find and create content through Domo's navigation bar and main menu, personalize your navigation with pins, and organize your pins with folders and smart folders.

---

## Access Domo's Navigation

You can find Domo Navigation on the left side of your browser window from every screen in Domo—except full-screen editors like those in Workflows or App Studio.

<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_01.png)</Frame>

By default, the navigation displays only the icon for each item.

To expand the navigation bar and show all icons with their titles, select the Domo icon at the bottom of the navigation or any empty space within the navigation bar.

<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_02.png)</Frame>

## Lock the Navigation Bar

When the Domo Navigation is unpinned, it hovers over the rest of the screen. When you select the pin icon in the right corner of the expanded navigation, it stays in the fixed location and does not cover any part of the screen.

To lock and unlock the expanded state of the navigation, select the pin icon <i className="icon-pin" aria-hidden="true" /> in the top right corner of the navigation bar.

<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_04.png)</Frame>

<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_05.png)</Frame>

<Note>
  **Note:** After you lock the navigation, it remains pinned throughout Domo
  until you select **Close** to collapse it.
</Note>

## Access and Create Content

In the Domo navigation bar, you can view your instance's logo, name, and URL, access and create content through the main menu, find your pinned items, and view your user profile.

### Find Your Instance's Logo, Name, and URL

Your instance's logo, name, and URL display at the top of the navigation.

<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_06.png)</Frame>

### Navigate the Main Menu

You can navigate Domo's features, create new content, view your most recent content, and access administrative tools in the main menu.

<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_07.png)</Frame>

#### Find Features

Select **Features** to find all of Domo's functionality by category.

<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_08.png)</Frame>

#### Search for Content

Locate any content in your Domo instance by entering a keyword in the **Search** bar. You can also browse for content by feature.

<Note>
  **Note:** The navigation's search function prioritizes content you have
  previously visited.
</Note>
<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_09.png)</Frame>

#### Create New Content

Select **Add** to access the **Create** menu and build new assets such as App Studio apps, cards, DataSets, workflows, and tasks. You can also connect and transform data, manage users and groups, and start conversations from this menu.

<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_09_02.png)</Frame>

#### View Recent Content

Select **Recent** to access content and features you've worked on lately, organized by timeframe.

<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_10.png)</Frame>

#### Access Administrative Settings

Select **Admin** to navigate all administrative tools, including governance, security, and feature management.

<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_11.png)</Frame>

### Find Your Pinned Items

When you pin an item, such as a Domo feature, App Studio app, card, or link, it shows under the **Your pins** section of the navigation. Learn how to [pin items to your Domo navigation](#pin-items).

  **Note:** When you pin multiple items, the navigation bar automatically
  adjusts. If the space is filled, you can access all your pinned items from a
  submenu, indicated by the **All navigation items**{" "}
  <i className="icon-apps" aria-hidden="true" /> icon. Select this icon to view
  the complete list and navigate to any of your pinned assets.
</Note>

## Personalize Your Navigation Experience

Pin your most-used features, like App Studio or DataFlows, or actions, like uploading a file or opening a card, to the **Your pins** section of the navigation, below the main menu.

### Pin Items

You can add any feature in the main menu to your pins by hovering over it and selecting the **Add to navigation** icon <i className="icon-pin" aria-hidden="true" />. You can pin any number of items to the **Your pins** section. The navigation scrolls as you add more items.

<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_15.png)</Frame>

### Personalize Your Navigation

You can pin any item in Domo to your navigation and manage your existing pins from the **Your pins** section.

To personalize your navigation, follow these steps:

1. In your navigation, go to the **Your pins** section.
2. Select **Edit**.

<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_16.png)</Frame>

A new tray opens in edit mode. In edit mode, you can do the following:

- **Reorder pins —** Drag pins to change their order.
- **Edit pin details —** Select the more options menu (the three-dot icon) on a pin.
- **Open pins in a new tab —** Open a pin in a new browser tab.
- **Remove pins —** Remove a pin from your navigation.

<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_17.png)</Frame>

3. To create and add a new pin to your navigation, select **ADD**.

<Frame>![Screenshot](/images/kb/left_nav_18.png)</Frame>

4. Choose one of the following from the **ADD** menu:
   - **Content item —** Pin Domo content that you own or that's shared with you in your instance, such as cards, dashboards, and DataSets.
   - **Feature —** Pin a Domo feature.
   - **Action —** Choose a specific action to take place when you select the pin. Options include uploading a file, creating a new ETL DataFlow, syncing on-premises data, and more.
   - **Website link —** Enter the URL that should open and whether to open it in a new browser tab.

     <Note>**Note:** The URL must begin with "**https://**".</Note>

   - To add a folder or a smart folder to your navigation, see [Organize Pinned Items in Folders](#organize-pinned-items-in-folders) and [Add a Smart Folder](#add-a-smart-folder).

5. Personalize your pin by selecting an icon, icon color, and background color.
6. Select **Done** to save your changes to your navigation.

### Organize Pinned Items in Folders

Group your pinned items into folders to keep your navigation organized. Folders can also contain subfolders.

To add a folder to your navigation, follow these steps:

1. In the **Your pins** section of the navigation, select **Edit**.

   <Frame>
     ![Edit option in the Your pins section of the
     navigation](/images/kb/nav_folders_1.png)
   </Frame>

   A tray opens in edit mode.

2. Select **ADD**.

   <Frame>![ADD option in the edit tray](/images/kb/nav_folders_2.png)</Frame>

3. Select **Folder** from the menu.

   <Frame>![Folder option in the ADD menu](/images/kb/nav_folders_3.png)</Frame>

4. Enter a folder name, and select an icon, icon color, and background color.

   <Frame>
     ![New folder modal with name, icon, icon color, and background color
     fields](/images/kb/nav_folders_4.png)
   </Frame>

5. Select **Create**.  
   The folder appears in your pins.

   <Frame>
     ![New folder in the Your pins section](/images/kb/nav_folders_5.png)
   </Frame>

To create a subfolder, follow the same steps inside a folder.

### Add a Smart Folder

Smart folders are dynamic. Instead of holding items you add by hand, a smart folder lists all of the content that matches its filter criteria. You can choose from the following smart folder options:

- **Owned by you —** Lists all the content you own.
- **Recents —** Lists all the content you've recently interacted with.
- **Apps & Dashboards —** Lists all the apps and dashboards you have access to.
- **Cards & KPIs —** Lists every chart, card, and KPI you have access to.
- **DataSets, DataSet Views, & Data Models —** Lists all the data you have access to.
- **Workspaces —** Lists every Workspace you created or that's shared with you.
- **DataFlows —** Lists all the DataFlows you have access to.

<Frame>![Smart folder options](/images/kb/smartfolders_1.png)</Frame>

Smart folders give you a consistent navigation experience across content types. For example, the apps and dashboards smart folder lists both App Studio apps and dashboards together, so all your content is in one place no matter which kind it is.

To add a smart folder to your navigation, follow these steps:

1. In the **Your pins** section of the navigation, select **Edit**.

   <Frame>
     ![Edit option in the Your pins section of the
     navigation](/images/kb/smartfolders_2.png)
   </Frame>

   A tray opens in edit mode.

2. Select **ADD**.

   <Frame>![ADD option in the edit tray](/images/kb/smartfolders_3.png)</Frame>

3. Select **Smart Folder** from the menu.

   <Frame>
     ![Smart Folder option in the ADD menu](/images/kb/smartfolders_4.png)
   </Frame>

4. Choose a smart folder option, and select **Next**.
5. (Optional) Customize the smart folder's name, icon, icon color, and background color.

   <Frame>
     ![Smart folder name, icon, and color
     settings](/images/kb/smartfolders_5.png)
   </Frame>

6. Select **Save** to create the smart folder.  
   The smart folder appears in your pins.

   <Frame>
     ![New smart folder in the Your pins section](/images/kb/smartfolders_6.png)
   </Frame>

When you open a smart folder, it lists all the content that matches its criteria and that you have access to. You can search the list and change its sort order.

<Frame>
  ![Open smart folder with search and sort
  options](/images/kb/smartfolders_7.png)
</Frame>

### Edit/Unpin Items

You can edit any item in the **Your pins** section in one of the following ways:

- **Edit a pinned item —** Select the more options menu (the three-dot icon) on the item to edit it.
- **Use edit mode —** Reorder pins, personalize pins, remove them from the navigation, change their location, or open them. To enter edit mode, select **Edit** in the **Your pins** section. When you finish, select **Done** to save your changes.
