## Intro

This article explains how to reassign DataFlow ownership from the Data Center, both for a single DataFlow and for multiple DataFlows at once, and the ownership and sharing limitations to keep in mind.

You can change the owner of a DataFlow in the Details view for that DataFlow. **You can do this *only* if you have an "Admin" security role (whether or not you are already the owner).** For more information about security roles, see Security Role Reference.

You can DataSet owners singly or in bulk.

<Note>
  **Note:** For a non-Admin user to see a DataFlow, that user must have share access to ALL of the DataSets powering the DataFlow.
</Note>

**To change the owner of a single DataFlow,**

1. Click **Data** in the toolbar at the top of the screen.
2. Click
   <i className="icon-dataflow sm" aria-hidden="true" /> in the left-hand navigation pane.
3. Locate and click the DataFlow you want to assign a new owner to.
     
   You can use the filter options to narrow down the DataFlows in the tab.
4. Click the pencil icon next to the name of the current owner.
     
   <Frame><img alt="" src="/images/kb/ka05w00000124TI-00N5w00000Ri7BU-0EM5w000005vOsU.png"/></Frame>
5. Select the new owner in the list.

**To change the owner of multiple DataFlows at once,**

1. Click **Data** in the toolbar at the top of the screen.
2. Click
   <i className="icon-dataflow sm" aria-hidden="true" /> in the left-hand navigation pane.
3. Locate one of the DataFlows you want to change the owner of.
     
   For more information about searching and filtering DataFlow in the Data Center, see [Data Center Layout](https://domo-support.domo.com/s/article/360047553253?language=en_US).
4. Mouse over the row for the DataFlow and click the circled checkmark that pops up over the connector icon.
     
   <Frame><img alt="data_center_dataflows_bulk.png" src="/images/kb/ka05w00000124SK-00N5w00000Ri7BU-0EM5w000005vOsL.png"/></Frame>
5. In the blue bar that appears at the top of the screen, click the
   <img src="/images/kb/ka05w00000124SK-00N5w00000Ri7BU-0EM5w000005vOsq.png" style={{width: 20, height: 20, display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'start', margin: '0'}}/> icon.
6. Select **Change Owner**.
     
   <Frame><img alt="data_center_bulk_change_owner.png" src="/images/kb/ka05w00000124TI-00N5w00000Ri7BU-0EM5w000005vOsV.png"/></Frame>
7. Select the new owner for the selected DataFlows and click **Reassign**.

All of the DataFlows you have selected should now be reassigned to the owner you have selected.

## Ownership and Sharing Limitations

Keep the following current-state behaviors in mind when managing DataFlow ownership and access:

- **Ownership is assigned to a single user.** A DataFlow has one owner at a time. When you reassign ownership, you select a single new owner—you cannot set a group as the owner. To give a team access, share the DataFlow (and its underlying DataSets) rather than transferring ownership.
- **Only an Admin can transfer ownership.** Changing the owner requires the **Admin** security role, whether or not you are the current owner. A non-Admin owner cannot reassign their own DataFlow.
- **Non-Admin visibility depends on DataSet access.** As noted above, a non-Admin user can see a DataFlow only if they have share access to *all* of the DataSets powering it.
