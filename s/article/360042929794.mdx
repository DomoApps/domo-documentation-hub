---
title: "Klout Connector"
excerpt: "Discontinued Klout connector reference; the Klout service has been retired and existing DataSets no longer run."
---

Lithium has made the decision to discontinue the Klout service. Due to this change, the Klout connector will no longer be usable and all Klout DataSets will cease to run.
  
Please reach out to Support if you have any questions. Or, you can reach out to Klout directly via [KloutQuestions@lithium.com](mailto:KloutQuestions@lithium.com "mailto:KloutQuestions@lithium.com").
