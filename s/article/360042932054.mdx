title: "Microsoft Excel Connector"
excerpt: "The Microsoft Excel Connector is deprecated as of the February 2018 release; use the File Upload Connector to import Excel files into Domo."
---

As of our February 2018 release, this connector is deprecated, having been replaced by the File Upload connector. For information about this connector, please visit the article for [File Upload Connector](/s/article/360043436573 "File Upload Connector").
