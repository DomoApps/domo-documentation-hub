---
title: "Set the Time Zone for a Workbench 5 Job"
excerpt: "Apply the Shift Date Timezone transform to a Workbench 5 DataSet job to convert datetime values to UTC before upload."
---

All datetime data coming into Domo is presumed to be UTC. You can set the time zone for a DataSet Job in Workbench, which will convert the time back to UTC before transmitting to Domo.

American Daylight Savings is taken into account for time zones in Workbench, but you may see the gaining or losing of an hour in your data depending on the time of year.

You apply a time zone to a DataSet using the **Shift Date Timezone** Transform. Note the UTC offset in the example below. In this case, 7 hours would be added to your datetime value in order to bring it inline with UTC.

<Frame><img alt="wb5_transforms_shift_timezone.png" src="/images/kb/ka05w00000123t1-00N5w00000Ri7BU-0EM5w000005vPP1.png"/></Frame>

<Steps titleSize="h2">
<Step title="Open the Database View">
In Workbench, select <i className="legacy-icon-database sm" aria-hidden="true" /> in the left-hand icon bar.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the DataSet Job">
In the Jobs listing, double-click the DataSet job with columns you want to filter.
</Step>
<Step title="Expand the Transforms Section">
Select **Transforms** to expand that section of the pane.
</Step>
<Step title="Add the Transform">
In the **Add a transform** menu, select **Filter Transform**.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the Transform Editor">
Select the <img src="/images/kb/ka05w00000123sv-00N5w00000Ri7BU-0EM5w000005vPOn.png" style={{width: 20, height: 20, display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'start', margin: '0'}}/> button.

The **Filter Transform Editor** appears.
</Step>
<Step title="Select the Time Zone">
In the **Data Timezone** menu, select the desired time zone.
</Step>
<Step title="Apply the Transform">
Select **Apply**.
</Step>
<Step title="Save the Transform">
Select the save icon <i className="icon-save sm" aria-hidden="true" /> at the top of the pane to save your transform.
</Step>
</Steps>
