## Intro

This article explains how to set up Okta as a SAML 2.0 identity provider for Domo, configure the Domo app integration, and enable single sign-on (SSO) for your users.

<Note>
   **Note:** This article assumes a working knowledge of Okta and focuses on the configuration parameters specific to setting up SAML in Domo. For general guidance on creating and configuring app integrations in Okta, see [Okta's documentation](https://help.okta.com/).
</Note>

---

## Required Grants

To set up SAML SSO with Okta, the following grant must be enabled for your role:

- **Manage All Company Settings —** Manage company metadata, security rules and SSO, publishing, and licenses.

Users with the "Admin" default security role have this grant by default. 

Learn more about [grants](/s/article/360043438973).

## Prerequisites

If you don't already have an Okta tenant, sign up for one at [okta.com](https://www.okta.com) and complete the activation email Okta sends you.

## Set Up Okta in Domo

1. In the Okta Admin Console, create a new SAML 2.0 app integration for Domo.

   <Note>
     **Note:** A prebuilt Domo app is available in the Okta App Integration Catalog and can be used instead of creating a new app integration. However, the catalog app may not support all the current capabilities of Domo SSO.
   </Note>

2. Configure the SAML settings using the values from Domo's SAML setup page (**Admin Settings** > **Security** > **SAML**). At minimum, set the [SAML Assertion Endpoint URL](/s/article/000005827#information-your-idp-may-need) and [Domo Entity ID](/s/article/000005827#information-your-idp-may-need), with **Name ID format** set to **EmailAddress** and **Application username** set to **Email**.

3. (Optional) Configure additional settings.
   - Learn more about [configuring additional settings in Domo](/s/article/000005827#configure-sso-settings).
   - Learn more about [sending user group membership to Domo](https://help.okta.com/en-us/content/topics/apps/define-group-attribute-statements.htm).

4. After you've created your app, [obtain the metadata URL out of Okta](https://support.okta.com/help/s/article/how-to-download-okta-identity-provider-metadata-and-saml-signing-certificates?language=en_US). 
   
   <Tip>
      **Tip:** Domo supports metadata upload, which is the recommended method to configure your SAML SSO in Domo. However, you can also configure your SAML in Domo manually using the Sign on URL, the Issuer, and the Signing Certificate from Okta.
   </Tip>

5. Now that you have the Okta SAML information, [configure SAML SSO in Domo](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/000005827#metadata-upload-or-manual-entry).

6. In Okta, assign users and groups to the Domo app on its **Assignments** tab.

   <Note>
     **Note:** A good best practice is to maintain one Okta group per client or team and assign that group to the Domo app. Adding or removing a user from the group grants or revokes their Domo access automatically.
   </Note>

7. (Optional) Create new users or groups in Okta as needed under **Directory**, and assign them to the Domo app.

## Access Domo with Okta SSO

Once SSO setup is complete, users can access Domo in either of the following ways:

* Sign in to Okta at `https://<your-org>.okta.com` and select the **Domo** tile on their dashboard.
* Sign in at the Domo instance URL and choose **Single Sign-On**. They are prompted for their Okta credentials (or signed in automatically if they already have an active Okta session).
