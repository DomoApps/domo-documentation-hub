## Intro

This article covers how to use Domo APIs to securely automate user and group management, manage data imports and exports, and extend Domo functionality.

---

Domo Application Program Interfaces (APIs) make it easier to administer your data and users, giving you the power and flexibility to get the most out of Domo. The data is encrypted in transit. Domo has a wide variety of APIs, and more are always being published.

Visit the Domo Developer Portal to see our [API documentation](/portal/API-Reference/overview).
