## Intro

Domo's AI & Data Science capability area goes beyond standard dashboards and visualization to let you build predictive models, run custom Python and R scripts, explore data with natural language, and manage AI agents—all inside the same environment as the rest of your data. This article explains the AI & Data Science pillar and points to the key articles for each component.

---

## Required Grants

Required grants vary by feature. Common grants for AI and data science work:

- **Edit Cards —** Required to use the Beast Mode AI Assistant and AI Chat on cards.
- **Manage All Company Settings —** Required to configure AI Service Layer settings and manage AI models (Admin grant).

Learn more about [grants](/s/article/360043438973).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Do I need to know Python or R to use Domo's AI features?">
  No. Most Domo AI features—AI Chat, Beast Mode AI Assistant, Magic ETL + AI tiles, AI Agents—require no code. Jupyter Workspaces and AutoML are optional tools for data scientists who want to go deeper with custom models and scripts.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between AutoML and Jupyter Workspaces?">
  AutoML is a no-code model-building tool: you point it at a DataSet, select the target column, and Domo trains a predictive model automatically. Jupyter Workspaces gives you a full Python and R environment for custom analysis and model training. Use AutoML for quick predictive models; use Jupyter for custom data science workflows.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I connect AI Chat to my own DataSets?">
  Use AI Readiness to add descriptions, synonyms, and context to any DataSet you want AI Chat to understand. Then open AI Chat and select the DataSet. See [AI Readiness](/s/article/000005561).
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
