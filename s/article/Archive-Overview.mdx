## Intro

The Archive is the section of the Knowledge Base for documentation about features that Domo has deprecated, retired, or replaced with a newer tool. These articles stay available as a reference for customers who still run a legacy version, such as Workbench 4, and for anyone who needs to understand old content before migrating it. This article explains what the Archive contains, points to the current replacement for each archived feature, and lists the archived articles by area.

The Archive is organized into four groups:

- **Deprecated Features —** Features, connectors, plugins, and chart types that are deprecated or retired.
- **Legacy Magic ETL —** Reference material for older Magic ETL environments.
- **Legacy Products —** Products that Domo no longer actively develops.
- **Legacy Workbench —** Documentation for Workbench 4 and for migrating from it to Workbench 5.

---

## Before You Use an Archived Article

Archived articles describe features as they worked when they were current. Screenshots, menu paths, and steps may no longer match the Domo interface, and a deprecated feature may stop working entirely on its retirement date. Where a supported replacement exists, use it for new work.

<Note>
  **Note:** If you can't find what you need in an archived article, contact your Customer Success Manager (CSM) or [Domo Support](/s/article/000005616).
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Why does Domo keep documentation for deprecated features?">
Some customers still run a legacy version of a tool, such as Workbench 4, or have older content built with a deprecated feature. The archived articles help them maintain that content and plan a migration to the supported replacement.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I still use a deprecated feature?">
It depends on the feature. Some deprecated features keep working until a stated retirement date, and others have already been retired. Check the notice at the top of each archived article, and use the replacement listed in [Find the Current Replacement](#find-the-current-replacement) for new work.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I move content from a legacy feature to its replacement?">
Start with the migration article for that feature. For example, see [Convert Content from MS Office Legacy Plugins to MS Office Domo Add-ins](/s/article/000005409) for Office content, or [Migrating from Workbench 4 to Workbench 5: Troubleshooting Guide](/s/article/7561566413463) for Workbench jobs.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
