## Intro

This article explains how to choose among Domo's options for sharing content with people outside your instance: public embed, the three private embed options, Domo Publish, and the Embed Edit Experience.

---

## Access

Enabling Domo Embed requires an Admin default security role or a custom role with the **Embed Cards** grant. Learn more about [grants](/s/article/360043438973).

## Compare the Options

| Option                         | Authentication  | PDP and personalized filters | Best for                                                                 |
| ------------------------------ | --------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Public embed                   | None            | Not supported                | Openly shareable, view-only content; not available on HIPAA instances    |
| Private embed — User-Based     | SSO             | Supported                    | Small, consistent groups of named users                                  |
| Private embed — Server-Based   | Server-side     | Programmatic filtering       | Large or transient audiences; greatest flexibility                       |
| Private embed — Platform-Based | SSO             | Supported                    | A fully editable embedded experience                                     |
| Domo Publish                   | Domo login      | Per-subscriber filtering     | Sharing curated pages and cards with other Domo instances                |
| Embed Edit Experience          | Identity Broker | Supported                    | Letting external users create alerts, schedule reports, and connect data |

## Understand Each Option

- **Public embed** is view-only. Because viewers don't log in, it doesn't support Personalized Data Permissions (PDP) or Alerts, and anyone with the URL can view. It is unavailable on HIPAA-compliant instances.
- **Private embed — User-Based** supports SSO and PDP and creates user licenses in Domo. It is recommended for small, consistent user groups.
- **Private embed — Server-Based** uses programmatic server-side filtering and works well for large or transient user groups. It is licensed by impressions or card loads rather than named users.
- **Private embed — Platform-Based** supports SSO and PDP and provides a completely editable experience.
- **Domo Publish** replicates pages and cards from a publisher instance into a dedicated subscriber instance, and lets you filter data for each subscriber before it is published.
- **Embed Edit Experience** uses the Domo Identity Broker—which supports SAML2, OIDC, JWT, or OAuth2—to authenticate and route users, and lets external customers create their own alerts, schedule their own reports, and connect their own data.

<Note>**Note:** Both the User-Based and Platform-Based private embed options require SSO enabled in Domo and on the destination website, portal, or application. Server-Based embed does not.</Note>

## Match the Option to Your Situation

- Publicly shareable, view-only content, no login: **public embed** (non-HIPAA instances only).
- A known set of external users who each need row-level security: **private User-Based embed**.
- A large or changing external audience with server-side filtering: **private Server-Based embed**.
- Sharing curated dashboards with a partner or customer who has their own Domo instance: **Domo Publish**.
- External customers who need to build and manage their own content: **Embed Edit Experience**.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Which options support Personalized Data Permissions?">
  The private embed options (User-Based, Server-Based, and Platform-Based) support personalized filtering—PDP for the authenticated options, and programmatic server-side filtering for Server-Based. Public embed does not, because viewers are not authenticated.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use public embed on a HIPAA instance?">
  No. Public embedding is unavailable for HIPAA-compliant Domo instances. Use one of the private embed options instead.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
