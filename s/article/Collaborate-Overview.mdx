## Intro

This article covers Domo's Collaborate tools—the features you use to share dashboards and cards within Domo, discuss data with your team, automate recurring updates, and track work. To export, embed, or publish content for audiences outside Domo, see [Distribute Content Outside Domo](/s/article/Choose-How-to-Share-Outside-Domo) in the Distribute pillar.

---

## Required Grants

Required grants vary by feature. Common grants for sharing and collaboration:

- **Edit Pages —** Share dashboards with other users.
- **Manage All Company Settings —** Required to configure company-wide sharing settings (Admin grant).

Learn more about [grants](/s/article/360043438973).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between a Scheduled Report and sharing content outside Domo?">
  A Scheduled Report sends a PDF snapshot of a dashboard by email on a schedule—it's one-way, time-triggered, and part of the Collaborate tools. To give external stakeholders an ongoing, read-only view (Publication Groups) or to export and embed content, use the Distribute tools, starting with [Distribute Content Outside Domo](/s/article/Choose-How-to-Share-Outside-Domo).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I share a card or dashboard with someone who doesn't have a Domo account?">
  Yes—through the Distribute tools rather than Collaborate. Publication Groups give external stakeholders a read-only view without a full Domo license, and exporting to PDF requires no Domo account at all. See [Distribute Content Outside Domo](/s/article/Choose-How-to-Share-Outside-Domo).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I embed a Domo dashboard in an external website or app?">
  Yes, using the Distribute tools. See [Embed Content Outside of Domo](/s/article/360043437993).
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
