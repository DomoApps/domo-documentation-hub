## New Features and Enhancements

### App Studio: App Components

A set of App Component updates lets you build polished, data-driven apps with no code: rules-based conditional formatting, a new Numeric widget, collapsible Sections, and built-in Group By and aggregations.

#### Conditional Formatting

Make your apps react to your data. App Components previously supported only static styling, so an "At Risk" project looked exactly like one that was "On Track" unless you built a custom solution. Conditional formatting brings rules-based styling directly into App Studio, with no pro-code required.

Set threshold rules — greater than, less than, or between — on numeric, text, and date fields, then choose how each widget responds: font color, bold or underline styling, pill fill and text, icons, or even showing and hiding widgets entirely. It's a natural fit for project trackers, pipeline boards, and status dashboards, where viewers need to see priority, status, and risk at a glance.

Rules can also reference a different column than the one being styled. For example, when Margin is above 20% for a city, you can highlight that city's Total Sales in blue. Rules are evaluated in the order they're listed, so you stay in control of exactly how your apps flag what matters.

Learn more about [applying conditional formatting in App Components](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/Apply-Conditional-Formatting-and-Conditional-Rendering-in-App-Components).

#### Group By and Aggregations

Stop building DataFlows just to summarize your data. App Components previously displayed row-level data, so creating a rolled-up view meant building a separate DataFlow or DataSet view — a process that could take hours. Now you can group and aggregate right inside the component.

Found in the new Group by category of the Filter pane, a multi-select dropdown lets you choose one or more columns to group by, then add aggregations to number widgets for a rich, summarized view. Once a group is in place, any additional text columns you add to a non-numeric widget are automatically appended to the Group By, keeping your view consistent as you build.

Learn more about [grouping and aggregating data in App Components](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/Group-and-Aggregate-Data-in-App-Components).

#### Numeric Widget

Build modern, eye-catching KPIs without writing a single Beast Mode. The new Numeric widget lets you pick any column from your DataSet and apply an aggregation directly on the widget, including Sum, Average, Count, Min, Max, and Distinct Count.

Filter each number by column or by relative date range — today, yesterday, last, previous, or next — and get started faster with three new KPI templates. All of the number formatting you already rely on, including decimals, prefixes and suffixes, and abbreviations, carries over, so you gain flexibility without giving anything up.

#### Sections Component

Turn a long stream of cards into a clear, scannable story. The new Sections component lets you group related cards into named, visually distinct areas of an App Studio page, each with a title and an optional hero card to anchor the narrative.

Sections can be collapsed and expanded, so viewers can focus on the KPIs that matter most without scrolling past unrelated content. Builders get a native, no-code way to structure apps that previously required custom components or workarounds, and App Catalyst can use Sections to generate richer, better-organized apps automatically.

### App Studio: Report Builder

Report Builder lets you create customizable, print-ready PDF reports in App Studio, schedule them for automatic email delivery, and let embedded app users export them without leaving your portal.

#### PDF Reports and Scheduler

No matter how interactive your apps are, executives still want reports as PDFs — on their desk or in their inbox, on a predictable cadence. Report Builder gives you a fully customizable way to build pixel-perfect PDF reports directly in App Studio, without maintaining duplicate dashboards just for printing.

Build a report from scratch, from existing app pages, or from your existing email reports, then shape every page in a visual preview pane. And with the new PDF Scheduler, you can configure a report once and have it delivered automatically, instead of opening, downloading, and attaching it by hand each week.

Highlights include:

- Customizable headers and footers with smart text for page numbers, dates, and more, so every report looks polished and consistent
- Page-level control to add or remove pages and to resize, rearrange, or remove content on any page from the preview pane
- Clean table pagination, with tables flowing across pages with sticky headers so content never gets cut off mid-row
- Filtered report views that let you create different versions of the same report for different audiences
- Scheduled PDF delivery that emails a saved report view as a PDF attachment on a recurring schedule
- One-click actions that add a button to any App Studio app to download a PDF or email a report

Learn more about [App Studio Report Builder](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/000005829).

#### Report Exports in Embedded Apps

Embedded App Studio apps can now deliver Report Builder PDFs, so your end users get the report without leaving your portal or product. Before, getting a PDF out of an embed meant leaving the experience or asking someone with Domo access to export it.

In private embeds, users can download a PDF with one click or use Send Now to share a report, both through button interactions you add to your app. Public embeds get an improved print-to-PDF option, so every embedded experience has a way to produce a clean report.

### Calculated Columns Rule Builder

Build conditional columns without writing a line of SQL. The Calculated Columns Rule Builder is a visual, no-code tool for creating conditional columns, now generally available everywhere calculated fields are created, including Analyzer, App Studio, and Beast Mode Manager. Instead of typing out complex CASE WHEN formulas in DQL (Domo's Beast Mode SQL), you construct guided IF/THEN rules using intuitive dropdown menus.

With the Rule Builder, you stack rules top to bottom: select a Text, Number, or Date column, choose an operator such as Is, Is between, or Is in, specify your output values, and set a catch-all Else rule at the end. Rules higher in the list take priority. Because rules are saved as JSON, your columns remain fully editable in the visual builder at any time rather than locking into raw SQL, and you can keep a calculation on the card or save it to the DataSet so others can reuse it.

### Cloud Integrations Query DomoStats Report

Gaining complete visibility into your cloud integration performance is now easier than ever. The new Cloud Integrations Query DomoStats report gives you an instant, data-driven window into your cloud data warehouse workflows.

Keep your pipelines running at peak performance with comprehensive reporting that tracks total query volume, execution times, cache hit rates, and error rates, all in one centralized view. By combining proactive performance insights with deep operational transparency, you can optimize your queries, troubleshoot faster, and make sure your cloud data ecosystem delivers maximum value.

Learn more about the [DomoStats Connector](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/360043433813).

### Dataset-Level Caching Settings

Some datasets change every few minutes while others rarely change at all. Caching was previously set at the Cloud Integration level, so every dataset from that integration shared the same cache time-to-live (TTL), and tuning one meant creating and maintaining a separate integration. Otherwise, teams over-refreshed slow-changing data and paid for unnecessary queries, or under-refreshed fast-changing data and got stale results.

The new Data Caching tab in your Cloud Integration's settings lets you set a TTL for each dataset individually, separate from the integration that sources it. This update lets you:

- Match caching to the data by setting different TTLs for different datasets without duplicating integrations
- Cut query costs by not refreshing slow-changing data that doesn't need it
- Keep fast-changing data fresher by tuning a dataset's TTL independently of the rest of the integration
- Avoid wasted consumption, because Domo checks freshness only when a dataset is queried and its TTL has expired, so idle data doesn't trigger redundant checks

Learn more about [configuring data freshness and caching in Cloud Integrations](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/Configure-Data-Freshness-and-Caching-in-Cloud-Integrations).

### Domo Everywhere Enhancements

New Domo Everywhere updates give admins more control over their embedded ecosystem, from customizing features across subscriber instances to permanently retiring embed links they no longer need.

#### Embed Link Deletion

Turning off an embed doesn't make it disappear. Disabled embed links pile up over time, and an old link that was once shared stays in the system indefinitely. Now admins can permanently delete embed links for cards, dashboards, and App Studio apps directly from the Embed Manager, using the same bulk select-and-edit workflow as Update Embed Type and Change Embed Owner.

To prevent accidental removal of a link still in use, deletion is only available for embeds that are already turned off. If you embed that content again later, a brand-new embed link is generated, so a deleted link can never be reactivated.

#### Feature Management in Instance Templates

Managing features across dozens or hundreds of subscriber instances is hard when you're limited to the settings available in the UI. Expanded feature management in Instance Templates gives you visibility into more features you can enable or disable across your subscriber instances. Build custom template configurations that tailor the experience for each of your customers, then roll them out at scale.

Need a feature that isn't listed? Reach out to your Domo account team.

Learn more about [using Instance Templates](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/000005786).

### Magic ETL Enhancements

Magic ETL's latest updates let you process unstructured file collections and generate accurate queries and calculations using everyday natural language, all without leaving your data pipeline.

#### Documents Input Tile

Processing unstructured file content such as PDFs, images, and Office documents used to require downloading files and handling transformations outside Domo, breaking pipeline automation, governance, and lineage. The Documents Input Tile brings native unstructured data processing directly into Magic ETL.

Located in the Documents section of the tile palette, this tile lets you select any Document Collection as an ETL input with auto-populated, customizable column mappings. By integrating file-based content into your automated data flows, you can eliminate manual hand-offs, combine file metadata with structured datasets, and streamline content delivery for downstream workflows and AI Agent Knowledge.

Learn more about the [Documents tiles in Magic ETL](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/Magic-ETL-Tiles-Documents).

#### Text-to-SQL and Text-to-Formula

Translating complex queries and calculation needs into precise syntax used to mean spending valuable time writing and troubleshooting raw code or relying on technical experts. The AI-powered Text-to-SQL and Text-to-Formula capabilities in Magic ETL bring natural language processing directly into your data pipelines, lowering the barrier to entry for everyone.

Describe your query or calculation needs in plain language behind the purple sparkle in the SQL editing pane, or within the expanded formula editors in Add Formula, Filter Rows, and Group By. By instantly generating accurate SQL and formulas from everyday text, you can eliminate technical bottlenecks, reduce errors, and accelerate your data workflows without ever leaving Magic ETL.

### Pro-Code App Enhancements

New pro-code app updates let developers build coordinated multi-app experiences with secure, targeted messaging between apps, and find the assets they need faster in a redesigned Asset Library.

#### App Broadcasting

Custom apps on the same Domo page previously had no supported way to talk to each other. Developers relied on page filters or Variables as workarounds, which affected every card on the page and created unintended side effects.

App Broadcasting solves this with a secure publish/subscribe system that lets apps send and receive messages on named channels. Clicking a row in a list app can load its details in a detail panel app, or a map app and a KPI card can react to the same user selection, all without page navigation or a page-level filter that affects every other card. App Broadcasting lets you:

- Coordinate apps precisely with two new domo.js methods to send and receive messages between specific apps
- Wire apps together visually in App Studio so they work together without writing integration code
- Define clear contracts by declaring publish and subscribe channels in manifest.json, so it's always explicit what each app sends and listens for
- Stay secure, since all messages route through Domo rather than directly between apps, and each one carries a verified sender ID that can't be spoofed

Learn more about [App Broadcasting](https://www.domo.com/docs/portal/Apps/App-Framework/Guides/broadcasting).

#### Asset Library Updates

As your library of images, icons, and brand assets grows, finding the right one can mean extra scrolling, clicking, and guesswork. New updates to the Asset Library refresh the home page with a cleaner, modern layout consistent with the rest of Domo. Clearer visual hierarchy and easier previews help you browse, find, and reuse assets faster, even as your library grows. Your existing assets and workflows are unchanged, so there is nothing new to learn or migrate.

Learn more about the [Asset Library](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/4403442101143).

### App Studio: Page Permissions

App Studio apps were previously all-or-nothing: everyone with access to an app saw every page. Giving audiences different information and access meant building and maintaining separate apps. Page Permissions change that by letting you set separate permissions for each page, right in the App Studio editor and with no code.

Turn on page permissions in the editor's app configuration, then use Share to choose which users or groups see each page. It uses Domo's existing Groups, so the groups you already have are ready to go. Executives can see financials while analysts see operational detail in the same app, and neither sees what they shouldn't. You manage page access alongside the rest of the app, so one app is production-ready for multi-audience deployment from day one.

### Connectors: Schema Protection

Schema changes in upstream sources — unexpected column deletions, new additions, or datatype modifications — can silently break downstream dataflows, cards, apps, and chatbots, leaving content builders scrambling to trace lineage and fix broken pipelines. Schema Protection gives you proactive control over data integrity right inside your connector configurations.

Set rules to allow or disallow new columns, type changes, or column deletions during indexing. If a destructive change violates your rules, the connector fails fast, protecting your downstream assets and preventing corrupted or incomplete reporting.

### Integration Overview Page

When you create a new Cloud Integration, figuring out immediate next steps and finding documentation can cause unnecessary friction and slow down time-to-value. The new Integration Overview Page serves as a dedicated landing page designed to guide you right after setup.

Acting as your central hub to streamline onboarding, the page features integration metric cards tracking Total Query Volume, Total Query Time, Cache Hit Rate, and Error Rate, alongside a connection health card that verifies reachability, authentication, and the status of read, write, and transform operations with an on-demand Test Now action. Combined with a dedicated documentation and resources section, it delivers clear guidance, actionable insights, and direct access to key tools to help you accelerate adoption and extract value faster.

### MCP Toolkit: Domo Embed

Managing embeds across cards, pages, and apps often means repetitive configuration, tracking ownership, and cleaning up outdated content by hand. With the new MCP Toolkit for Embeds, AI assistants and agents handle that work for you.

Just ask an agent to create, update, reassign, or delete embeds using natural language. A universal orchestration skill routes each request to the right tool, guides you through configuration, and prompts you for any missing details. Every action respects your existing platform permissions, so you can speed up embed management and reduce sprawl without compromising governance.

Learn more about [connecting AI tools to Domo using MCP](https://www.domo.com/docs/s/article/Connect-AI-Tools-to-Domo-Using-MCP).

### Workspaces and Navigation

The left nav is now faster to customize, adds Smart Folders for live collections of your Domo content, and shows each person only the nav items they can access.

#### Nav Customization

Pin and organize content in your left nav in fewer steps. A new Add menu replaces the pin modal's content-type tabs with a single dropdown and puts Folder (formerly submenu) right beside it, instead of inside the Location settings. The same streamlined flow works whether you're editing your own nav or building a nav config for your organization, and it's where you'll add new pin types like Smart Folders.

#### Nav Smart Folders

Pinning content to your nav only goes so far: there was no way to surface a live list of content by type, like all the cards you can access or everything you own. Nav Smart Folders fill that gap with live collections of your Domo content, organized by type or ownership, right in your left nav and Workspaces.

Prebuilt Smart Folders cover dashboards, apps, cards, datasets, and more, plus personal views like Owned by Me, Created by Me, and Recents. Add one via Edit > Add > Smart Folder, or alongside folders in a Workspace as a read-only view. Each shows only content you have permission to see and updates automatically as content is created, shared, or removed, so you can find what you need in fewer clicks. Smart Folders work on web and mobile.

#### Permissions-Aware Nav Configs

Share one curated nav with everyone, and let permissions decide what each person sees. With permissions-aware nav configs, a pin appears only for users who can access its content. Admins can build one nav for a team or organization instead of separate versions for each access level, users get a cleaner nav with no dead-end links, and admins spend less time maintaining configs.
