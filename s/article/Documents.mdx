title: Documents

Documents open a new world of insights from images, PDF's, transcripts
and other types of unstructured data. Extract structured information
from images or customer reviews to power visualizations and Data
Products, automate document-driven business processes, or leverage AI
Search to ground AI Agent responses using non-public documentation.

## Creating a Document Collection
There are two options to bring your unstructured data into Domo. Upload
files from your computer or connect via one of our file connectors;
Amazon S3, SFTP, GitHub, Google Drive or Confluence, with more to come.

## Accessing Documents from an Agent -- Workflow
Agents can fully utilize Documents as a 'Tool' or 'Knowledge' within the
'AI Agent Task' inside WorkFlows. From the 'Tools' tab click the 'Add
Tools' button. Search for "Documents" and select the 'DOMO DOCUMENTS
package. Then choose the 'AI Search File Set' method.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="What types of documents are supported?">
All common image and document file types can be stored. AI Search
indexing is limited to the following file types; txt, md, csv, css,
json, html, doc, docx, xls, xlsx, pdf, jpg, jpeg, png.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the size limit per document?">
Images: 3.75MB

Documents: 50MB (\*manual upload limited to 10MB)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many documents can I have?">
Unlimited, consumption rates do apply...
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="If I have documents in a cloud service like Google Drive, can I easily connect them to Documents for automatic uploads like a Domo Connector?">
Yes, you can connect to Amazon S3, SFTP, GitHub, Google Drive or
Confluence, with more to come.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Once I have the documents uploaded, where do I find them? Can I access them with AI Chat?">
Agents can fully utilize Documents as 'Knowledge'.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I create visualization from a Document in a dashboard or app studio app?">
No, not at this time, this is on our roadmap.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I manage access to Documents, both for uploading permissions and for usage?">
Currently, permissions are based on the entire Document controlled
through Roles & Grants.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does consumption look like for Documents? Is it per upload? Per view? Per usage in other parts of the platform? Is there a storage cost?">
Yes, there are consumption charges for file storage, Agent Knowledge
storage & processing, see our online terms for full details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will I be able to use Documents with the Magic ETL Text Generation tile?">
Yes, this is on our roadmap.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
