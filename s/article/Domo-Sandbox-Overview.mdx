## Intro

Domo Sandbox gives your team a safe development environment for Domo content. Instead of making changes directly to your production instance—where any mistake immediately affects all users—you work in a separate Sandbox instance, version your changes, and promote them to production through a controlled review process. Sandbox supports pages, cards, DataFlows, App Studio apps, DataSet Views, and connectors. This article explains what Domo Sandbox is, how it works, and points to the configuration articles.

---

## Required Grants

To configure and use Domo Sandbox, the following grants must be enabled for your role:

- **Administer Sandbox —** Configure instance-level Sandbox settings, send and accept instance invitations, and manage all repositories.
- **Manage Repositories —** Create, commit, and share repositories.

Learn more about [grants](/s/article/360043438973).

## How Sandbox Works

Sandbox uses linked repositories to connect a development (Sandbox) Domo instance to a production instance. You make changes in the Sandbox instance, commit them to the repository, and when the changes are ready, promote them to production. The promotion process is version-controlled, so you can compare what changed before applying it.

Content types supported for Sandbox promotion:

- Pages (dashboards) and cards
- DataFlows (Magic ETL, SQL, Adrenaline)
- App Studio apps
- DataSet Views
- Connectors

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Is Domo Sandbox a completely separate Domo instance?">
  Yes. Domo Sandbox is a separate Domo environment with its own users, DataSets, and content. Linked repositories are the mechanism that connects Sandbox to production so you can promote approved changes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do DataSets and real data get copied to Sandbox?">
  Sandbox promotes the structure and configuration of content (DataFlows, cards, app layouts)—not the underlying data. Sandbox uses its own DataSets, which you configure independently from production. You can use sample or anonymized data in Sandbox while production runs on live data.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who should use Domo Sandbox?">
  Sandbox is intended for teams with active development on Domo content who need a way to test changes without risking production. It is most valuable when multiple developers are building DataFlows or apps simultaneously and need a controlled promotion process.
</Accordion>
