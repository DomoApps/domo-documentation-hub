## Intro

This article explains how to use Domo's GoTo Phone Connector to retrieve data from your GoTo Phone account. Use this connector to access call history, call queues, agents, and call outcome data for operational and performance reporting.

---

## Prerequisites

To connect to your GoTo Phone account and create a DataSet, you need your GoTo credentials.

This article covers the fields and menus specific to the GoTo Phone Connector user interface. For general information about adding DataSets, setting update schedules, and editing DataSet information, see [Add a DataSet Using a Data Connector](/s/article/360042926274).

## Configure the Connection

This section describes the options in the **Credentials** and **Details** panes on the GoTo Phone Connector page. The components of the **Scheduling** and **Name & Describe Your DataSet** panes are universal across most connector types and are discussed in [Add a DataSet Using a Data Connector](/s/article/360042926274).

### Enter Your Credentials

The GoTo Phone connector uses the OAuth mechanism to connect to GoTo. When you add an account for the first time, click **Connect**. The GoTo sign-in page appears, where you enter your account credentials and complete the authentication. After the account is authenticated, it appears in the **Credentials** list.

If you add multiple accounts, you can select the account that you want to use. If accounts already exist for the connector and you want to add another account, click **Add Account** in the drop-down list. Then enter the new account details and complete the authentication.     

<Note>**Note:**  If you change your account credentials, such as your password, you must reauthenticate the account. Otherwise, the dataset fails and displays an error indicating that the account credentials are invalid. </Note>

After you have entered valid credentials, you can use the same account any time you set up a new Domo-GoTo Phone connection. You can manage connector accounts in the **Accounts** tab in the Data Center. For more information about this tab, see [Manage User Accounts for Connectors](/s/article/360042926054).
