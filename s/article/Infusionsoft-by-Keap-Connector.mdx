## Intro

This article explains how to use Domo's Infusionsoft by Keap Connector to retrieve and analyze your marketing automation and sales data. You can combine Infusionsoft by Keap data with data from other systems to create customized dashboards and monitor customer relationships, marketing performance, lead capture, sales, and e-commerce activities in one view.

## Prerequisites

To connect to the Infusionsoft by Keap account and create a DataSet, you need the email address and password associated with your Infusionsoft by Keap account.

This article covers the fields and menus specific to the Infusionsoft by Keap Connector user interface. For general information about adding DataSets, setting update schedules, and editing DataSet information, see [Add a DataSet Using a Data Connector](/s/article/360042926274).

## Configure the Connection

This section describes the options in the **Credentials** and **Details** panes on the Infusionsoft by Keap Connector page. The components of the **Scheduling** and **Name & Describe Your DataSet** panes are universal across most connector types and are discussed in [Add a DataSet Using a Data Connector](/s/article/360042926274).

### Enter Your Credentials

The Domo Infusionsoft by Keap connector uses OAuth to connect, so there is no need to enter credentials within Domo. Select **Add Account** to open the Infusionsoft by Keap OAuth screen where you can enter your Infusionsoft by Keap credentials.

After you have entered valid credentials, you can use the same account any time to set up a new Domo-Infusionsoft by Keap connection. You can manage connector accounts in the **Accounts** tab in the Data Center. For more information about this tab, see [Manage User Accounts for Connectors](/s/article/360042926054).
