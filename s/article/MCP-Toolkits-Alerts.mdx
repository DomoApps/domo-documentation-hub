## Intro

The Alerts Toolkit lets AI create, update, and manage Domo alerts and subscriptions on cards and datasets. 

---

<Note>**Note:** A Toolkit's tools only return data and perform actions that the connected Domo account already has permission to access.</Note>

## Tools Included

- Create Alert
- Update Alert
- Delete Alert
- Get Alert
- List Alerts
- Get Alert Series
- Subscribe to Alert
- Unsubscribe from Alert
- Update Alert Subscription
- Get Alert Subscriptions
- Search
