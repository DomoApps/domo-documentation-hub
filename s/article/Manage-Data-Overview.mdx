## Intro

Once data is in Domo, you need tools to govern it: find DataSets, update their owners, monitor freshness, share access, export, and eventually retire them. Domo's Manage Data capability area covers these day-to-day data operations through the Data Center and a set of lifecycle actions available on every DataSet. This article explains what Manage Data covers and points to the key articles for each task.

---

## Required Grants

To manage DataSets in Domo, the following grants must be enabled for your role:

- **Can Create DataSets —** Create, modify, and delete DataSets you own.
- **Manage All DataSets —** View, edit, and manage DataSets owned by other users (typically an Admin-only grant).

Learn more about [grants](/s/article/360043438973).

## Data Center

The Data Center is the central hub for all DataSets in your Domo instance. From the Data Center you can search, filter, and sort DataSets; view their status, owner, and update schedule; and take management actions.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Who can see all DataSets in the Data Center?">
  By default, users only see the DataSets they own or have been shared. Users with the Manage All DataSets grant (typically Admins) can see and manage every DataSet in the instance. See [Data Center Layout](/s/article/360047553253).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I transfer all DataSets from a departing user to a new owner?">
  From the Data Center, filter DataSets by owner, select all, and use the bulk-action menu to change the owner. You can also change ownership on individual DataSets from the Options menu. See [Change the Owner of a DataSet](/s/article/360042926074).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between deleting a DataSet and disconnecting a card?">
  Deleting a DataSet permanently removes it and breaks all cards, DataFlows, and alerts that depend on it. Disconnecting or reconnecting a card (via [Connecting Cards to a Different DataSet](/s/article/360043430653)) leaves the DataSet intact and just changes which DataSet the card reads from.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
