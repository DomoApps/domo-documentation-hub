## Intro

This article covers the lifecycle tasks for an App Studio app: deleting an app, finding and cleaning up the orphaned cards a deletion leaves behind, and using Domo Sandbox to version and roll back app changes.

## Delete an App

To delete your app, select <i className="icon-trash sm" aria-hidden="true" /> **Delete**.

A dialog displays, confirming that deleting the app also deletes its pages and app-specific elements.

To complete app deletion, select **Continue**. Select **Cancel** to return to the **App configuration** panel.

## Manage Cards behind an App

Deleting an app removes the app and its pages, but the cards, Domo Bricks, and forms placed in it remain in your Domo instance. These cards become *orphaned*—they are no longer attached to an app, but they still exist and still count toward your instance's content. Domo does not prompt you to delete these cards when you delete the app, and there is no option to delete the app together with its cards.

Orphaned cards can be difficult to locate after the fact. An App Studio app page is not listed in a card's **Move or Copy** menu, and it does not appear in the card's info panel. As a result, you cannot trace a card back to its parent app page the way you can trace a card to a dashboard.

### Delete Orphaned Cards

An admin can find and remove orphaned cards from the instance-wide card list:

1. Select **More** in the toolbar at the top of the screen, then select **Admin**.
2. Select **Cards** in the left-hand pane. A list of all cards in your Domo instance displays.
3. Use the column filters to narrow the list to the cards you want to remove.
4. Select the cards you want to remove, then delete them.

You must have an "Admin" default security role or a custom role with the Manage All Cards, Pages and Apps (App Studio) grant enabled to use the **Admin > Cards** page. To learn more about grants, see [Manage User Roles and Grants](/s/article/360043438973). For more detail on working with the instance-wide card list, see [Moving or Copying Cards to Other Dashboards](/s/article/360042923254).

### Locate Orphaned Cards with the Governance Connector

To identify orphaned cards in bulk, you can use the Domo Governance Datasets connector. Its Cards report lists every card in your instance along with its owner and the pages it resides on, which you can use to find cards that are no longer tied to active content. See [Find Which Dashboard a Card Lives On](/s/article/Find-Which-Dashboard-a-Card-Lives-On).

## Version and Roll Back an App with Sandbox

App Studio does not keep a built-in version history. To version an app and roll back to an earlier state, use [Domo Sandbox](/s/article/4403367344023) as the version-control path for apps: commit an app to a Sandbox repository, promote versions between development and production instances, and restore an earlier version if a change needs to be reverted. Undo and redo within a single editing session are available in the editor header—see [Use Advanced Saving Options](/s/article/Build-an-App-Studio-App#use-advanced-saving-options-in-app-studio).

For the full promotion, rollback, and governance workflow, see [Domo Sandbox](/s/article/4403367344023).
