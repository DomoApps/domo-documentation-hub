## Intro

This article explains how to use Domo's Podium OAuth connector to analyze your online review data in Domo.

---

## Prerequisites

To connect to your Podium OAuth account and create a DataSet, you must have your Podium credentials.

For more information about the Podium API, see the [Podium API Documentation](https://podium.readthedocs.io/en/latest/API.html).

This article covers the fields and menus specific to the Podium OAuth Connector user interface. For general information about adding DataSets, setting update schedules, and editing DataSet information, see [Add a DataSet Using a Data Connector](/s/article/360042926274).

## Configure the Connection

This section describes the options in the **Credentials** and **Details** panes on the Podium OAuth Connector page. The components of the **Scheduling** and **Name & Describe Your DataSet** panes are universal across most connector types and are discussed in [Add a DataSet Using a Data Connector](/s/article/360042926274).

### Enter Your Credentials
                                                        
The Domo Podium OAuth connector uses the OAuth mechanism to connect to Podium. When you add an account for the first time, click **Connect**. The Podium OAuth sign-in page appears, where you enter your account credentials and complete the authentication. After the account is authenticated, it appears in the **Credentials** list.<br/><br/> If you add multiple accounts, you can select the account that you want to use.
If accounts already exist for the connector and you want to add another account, click **Add Account** in the drop-down list. Then enter the new account details and complete the authentication.
<br/><br/> <Note>**Note:**  If you change your account credentials, such as your password, you must reauthenticate the account. Otherwise, the dataset fails and displays an error indicating that the account credentials are invalid. </Note> <br/><br/>
After you have entered valid credentials, you can use the same account any time you set up a new Podium OAuth connection. You can manage connector accounts in the **Accounts** tab in the Data Center. For more information about this tab, see [Manage User Accounts for Connectors](/s/article/360042926054).
