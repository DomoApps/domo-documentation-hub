## Intro

This article explains whether you can restore a deleted dashboard, what happens to a dashboard's subpages when it is deleted, and how to protect important dashboards from accidental loss.

---

## Can You Restore a Deleted Dashboard?

Domo does not provide a self-service way to restore a deleted dashboard. After a dashboard is deleted, there is no button or setting in the interface that brings it back.

If a dashboard was deleted by accident, contact [Domo Support](https://domohelp.domo.com) as soon as possible to ask whether it can be recovered from a backup. Recovery is not guaranteed, and the sooner you reach out, the better the chance that a recent backup still contains the dashboard.

<Warning>**Important:** Treat dashboard deletion as permanent. Do not rely on Support recovery as a substitute for the prevention steps below.</Warning>

## What Happens When You Delete a Dashboard

Deleting a dashboard removes the dashboard and its layout. Keep the following in mind before you delete:

- **Deleting a parent dashboard also deletes its subpages.** If a dashboard has subpages beneath it, deleting the parent removes those subpages as well. Move or copy any subpages you want to keep before deleting the parent.
- **Cards are not necessarily deleted with the dashboard.** A card can appear on more than one dashboard, so deleting a dashboard removes the card from that dashboard's layout but does not delete a card that also lives elsewhere. A card placed only on the deleted dashboard may no longer be surfaced in the interface.

## Prevent Accidental Deletion

Because deletion cannot be reliably undone, protect important dashboards before anything goes wrong:

- **Duplicate the dashboard.** Keep a copy of a critical dashboard so a deletion of the original does not lose the layout.
- **Limit who can edit and delete.** Share dashboards with the minimum access people need. A viewer cannot delete a dashboard; only owners and users with edit access can.
- **Export the content.** Export the cards or the underlying data so the information exists outside the dashboard.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Is there a trash or recycle bin for dashboards?">
  No. Domo does not have a trash or recycle bin that holds deleted dashboards for later restoration. Recovery, if any, is handled by Domo Support from a backup.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I deleted the parent dashboard. Are the subpages gone too?">
  Yes. Deleting a parent dashboard deletes its subpages along with it. Contact Domo Support to ask about backup recovery, and going forward, move or copy subpages you want to keep before deleting a parent.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I keep this from happening again?">
  Duplicate critical dashboards, restrict edit and delete access to the people who need it, and export the cards or underlying data so the information exists independently of the dashboard.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
