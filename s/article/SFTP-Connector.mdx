This article will walk you through setting up a connection to an SFTP server.

You will need the SFTP hostname, port, username, and password.

An SFTP passphrase is a security measure used to encrypt your private key on your local machine. It acts as an additional layer of protection, ensuring that even if your key file is stolen, it cannot be used without the passphrase. It is distinct from your regular server account password.
