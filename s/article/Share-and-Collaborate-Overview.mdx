## Intro

Getting the right insights to the right people is as important as building them. Domo's Share & Collaborate capability area covers the tools you use to share dashboards and cards, discuss data with your team, automate reports, and push content to external audiences. This article explains each tool and points to the key articles for each use case.

---

## Required Grants

Required grants vary by feature. Common grants for sharing and collaboration:

- **Edit Pages —** Share dashboards with other users.
- **Manage Publication Groups —** Required to create and manage Publication Groups (Admin-level feature).
- **Manage All Company Settings —** Required to configure company-wide sharing settings (Admin grant).

Learn more about [grants](/s/article/360043438973).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between a Scheduled Report and a Publication Group?">
  A Scheduled Report sends a PDF snapshot of a dashboard by email on a schedule—it's one-way and time-triggered. A Publication Group gives a defined set of recipients a persistent, read-only view of specific dashboards in Domo—they log in to see the content rather than receiving an email. Use Scheduled Reports for periodic snapshots; use Publication Groups for ongoing, persistent access.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I share a card or dashboard with someone who doesn't have a Domo account?">
  Yes, through Publication Groups or by exporting to PDF. Publication Groups give external stakeholders access to a read-only view without a full Domo license. Exporting and sharing as a PDF file requires no Domo account at all.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I embed a Domo dashboard in an external website or app?">
  Yes. Domo's embed capabilities let you embed cards and dashboards in external applications. See the Domo Everywhere section of the Developer Portal for embed options and implementation guides.
</Accordion>
