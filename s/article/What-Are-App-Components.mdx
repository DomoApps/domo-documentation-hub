## Intro

App components are reusable, DataSet-powered building blocks you add to an App Studio app to visualize and interact with your data. Instead of configuring a card from scratch, you drop a component onto the canvas—such as a gallery, list, banner, filter list, or KPI component—and point it at a DataSet. This article explains what app components are and links to the how-tos for creating and customizing them.

See [What Is App Studio?](/s/article/What-is-App-Studio) for the broader app-building context.

## What You Can Build with App Components

- **Galleries, lists, and banners —** Present records from a DataSet in configurable templates.
- **Filter lists —** Let app viewers filter page content from a list of values.
- **KPI components —** Surface a key metric with built-in formatting.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How are app components different from cards?">
  Cards are standalone visualizations you build in Analyzer and place on dashboards or in apps. App components are configurable templates—galleries, lists, banners, filter lists, and KPI components—added and configured directly on the App Studio canvas and pointed at a DataSet, without building a card first.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where do app components live?">
  App components are part of an App Studio app. See [Build an App Studio App](/s/article/Build-an-App-Studio-App) to learn how to add elements to the canvas.
</Accordion>
