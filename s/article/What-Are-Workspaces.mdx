## Intro

A Workspace is a shared container that brings a team's related content—dashboards, cards, apps, and DataSets—together in one place. Instead of sharing each item individually, you organize them in a Workspace and share the whole Workspace with your team in a single step. This article explains what Workspaces are and links to the how-to for creating and sharing them.

## What You Can Do with a Workspace

- **Organize related content —** Collect dashboards, cards, apps, and DataSets into one Workspace, and arrange them with folders and pins.
- **Find content faster —** Search within a Workspace to locate an item.
- **Share in one step —** Share a Workspace with your team instead of sharing each item separately.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How is a Workspace different from a dashboard or an app?">
  A dashboard or an App Studio app is a single piece of content. A Workspace is a container that groups related content—dashboards, cards, apps, and DataSets—so a team can find and share it together.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is a Workspace the same as a Jupyter Workspace?">
  No. A Jupyter Workspace is a data-science coding environment in the AI & Data Science area. The Workspace described here is a shared container for organizing and sharing Domo content. See [Jupyter Workspaces | User Guide](/s/article/36004740075) for the data-science feature.
</Accordion>
