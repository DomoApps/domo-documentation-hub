## Intro

Domo AI brings generative AI and machine learning capabilities directly into the Domo platform. You can use Domo AI to ask natural-language questions about your data, generate Beast Mode calculations, build AI-powered Workflows, and manage custom LLM and data-science models—without leaving your Domo environment. This article explains what Domo AI is, the key capabilities it provides, and points to the articles for each feature area.

---

## Required Grants

To access Domo AI, the following grants must be enabled for your role:

- **Use AI Services —** Access to features and Apps built using the AI Services Layer.
- **Use AI Chat —** Allows you to access and use the Domo AI Chat feature throughout Domo.
- **Manage Conversational Agents —** View, edit, and delete any Conversational Agent feature in this instance.
- **Manage Toolkits —** View, edit, and delete any Toolkit feature in this instance.

Learn more about [grants](/s/article/360043438973).

## Domo AI Pro

Domo AI Pro unlocks advanced AI capabilities through a consumption-based credit model. Credits are consumed per AI call, with rates varying by model tier.

- [Domo AI Pro](/s/article/000005918) — Capabilities, pricing, and credit usage overview.
- [AI Reporting](/s/article/000005851) — Monitor AI usage across your Domo instance using DomoStats AI reports.

## AI Chat

AI Chat lets you ask complex, multi-part questions about your data in plain language. It can answer questions that span multiple DataSets, analyze Documents and other unstructured data, and view or create visualizations from the conversation. You can also upload files, paste images, and @mention additional knowledge to give AI Chat more context.

## AI Agents

AI agents are autonomous AI workers you build in Domo to complete tasks—researching, reasoning, and taking actions across your Domo environment or external systems.

- [Getting Started With AI Agents](/s/article/000005853) — Roadmap for building and deploying AI agents in Domo.
- [AI Library](/s/article/AI-Library) — Build custom agents with AI Agent Builder and assemble the Toolkits that govern what agents can do.
- [Build an AI Agent Task in Workflows](/s/article/000005865) — Embed an AI agent as a step in a Domo Workflow.
- [Configure Agent Role and Instructions](/s/article/Configure-Agent-Role-and-Instructions)
- [AI Agents | Frequently Asked Questions](/s/article/000005720)

<Note>**Note:** Agents get their capabilities from Toolkits—packaged sets of tools, data, and instructions that define what an agent can do. Use **Domo-provided Toolkits**, such as the [Domo MCP](/s/article/Domo-MCP) Toolkit, for common use cases, or build **custom Toolkits** in the [AI Library](/s/article/AI-Library#create-a-toolkit) to fit your business.</Note>

## AI-Assisted Tools

Domo AI is embedded throughout the platform to assist with common tasks:

- **Beast Mode AI Assistant —** Describe the calculation you want in plain language; Domo generates the Beast Mode formula. See [Beast Mode AI Assistant](/s/article/000005304).
- **Magic ETL + AI —** Use AI-powered tiles in Magic ETL pipelines to classify, summarize, or enrich data using LLM calls. See [Magic ETL + AI](/s/article/Magic-ETL-AI).
- **AI in Workflows —** Integrate an AI Service Layer call as a step in a Domo Workflow. See [Workflows — AI Service Layer Integration](/s/article/000005369).

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Does Domo AI send my data to a third-party AI provider?">
  Domo AI uses Domo's own AI Service Layer, which can be configured to use Domo-hosted models, your own OpenAI or Anthropic keys, or cloud provider integrations (AWS Bedrock, Azure OpenAI, Google Vertex). What data is sent to each provider depends on your configuration. See [AI Service Layer Settings](/s/article/000005279) and [AI Adapters](/s/article/AI-Adapters-Databricks-OpenAI-Bedrock).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is Domo AI Pro and do I need it for AI Chat?">
  Domo AI Pro is an add-on tier that unlocks advanced AI features and higher usage limits through AI credits. Some basic AI features are included in standard Domo subscriptions. See [Domo AI Pro](/s/article/000005918) for a breakdown of what requires AI credits versus what is included by default.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I make AI Chat give better answers for my DataSets?">
  Use AI Readiness to add column descriptions, synonyms, and context to your DataSets. This improves how Domo AI interprets your data and generates more accurate answers. See [AI Readiness](/s/article/000005561).
</Accordion>
