## Intro

An alert is a rule you set on a visualization card or DataSet that tells Domo to notify you when the data changes in a way you care about—a metric drops below a target, a count exceeds a limit, or any condition you define. When the rule is met, Domo sends a notification by email, SMS, Domo Mobile push, or in-app pop-up. This article explains what alerts are, how to create them, and points to the key articles for managing them.

- **Edit Cards —** Required to set alerts on visualization cards.

Learn more about [grants](/s/article/360043438973).

## How Alerts Work

You define an alert by choosing a card or DataSet, selecting the metric to watch, and setting the trigger condition (for example, "Sales < 10,000" or "Row Count > 1,000,000"). Domo evaluates the condition every time the underlying DataSet updates. When the condition is met, all subscribers receive a notification through their configured channels.

Alerts can have multiple subscribers—anyone can follow an alert someone else created, and the alert owner can push notifications to a group or the entire company.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Who receives an alert when it triggers?">
  The alert owner and all subscribers receive notifications when the trigger condition is met. Subscribers can join an alert from the Alert Center or from the card detail view. Alert owners can also push a triggered alert to a group or the whole company. See [Manage Your Alerts](/s/article/360043430533).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How often does Domo evaluate alert conditions?">
  Domo evaluates alert conditions every time the underlying DataSet runs. The evaluation is triggered by the DataSet update, not on a fixed clock schedule.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I set an alert on a Beast Mode calculation?">
  Yes. When creating a card alert in Analyzer, you can select any Beast Mode calculation as the metric to watch, just as you would a standard column.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How are alerts affected by Personalized Data Permissions?">
  PDP policies can affect what data each subscriber sees in an alert notification, depending on how PDP is configured on the DataSet. See [PDP and Alerts](/s/article/360042934634) for details.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
